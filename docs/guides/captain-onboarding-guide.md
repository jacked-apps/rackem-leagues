# Captain's Guide — Getting Your Team Into the App

This is the step-by-step for captains. The whole job is: **share one link,
then approve each player as they sign up.** That's it. Follow along.

---

## The big picture (read this first)

You have **one link** for your team. Every player uses the same link. When a
player signs up through it, you get a request — you tap **Add**, and they're on
your roster. You're the doorman: you share the address, then you let people in.

You do **not** need anyone's email, you do **not** type in their info, and you
do **not** create accounts for them. They do that part themselves.

---

## Step 1 — Open "Invite my team"

1. On your phone or computer, open the app and **log in**.
2. Go to **My Teams** (it's in the menu).
3. Find your team and tap the **Invite my team** button.
4. A little box pops up. This is where your team's invite lives.

---

## Step 2 — Copy the invite message

1. In that box, tap the button that says **Copy invite message**.
2. That's it — the message is now copied and ready to paste. It already has your
   name, your team name, and your team's link written into it for you. **You
   don't have to type or write anything.**
3. (You'll also see a smaller **"Or just the link"** copy underneath. Ignore it —
   the full message is better because it tells your player exactly what to do.)

The message looks like this:

> **{your name}** has invited you to join **{your team}**!
>
> Tap this link to get on the roster: {link}
>
> You'll sign in with your email, fill out a quick profile, and tap "Add me."
> Then **{your name}** can approve and you're on the team.

---

## Step 3 — Paste it into a message your players will see

Now you've got that message copied. Send it to your players. Paste it into
**any message your player will actually see**:

- a **group text** to your whole team (this is the fastest — everybody at once), or
- a **text** to one player at a time, or
- **email**, Facebook Messenger, GroupMe, WhatsApp — whatever you and your team
  already use to talk.

It's the same message and the same link for everybody. Paste it in and hit send.
That's all the sharing you have to do.

---

## Step 4 — Wait for your players to follow the link

Now it's their turn — you just wait.

Each player taps the link in your message and follows the instructions on their
screen — they sign in with their email, fill out a few quick details, and tap a
button that says **"Add me."** The app walks them through every step. You don't
have to talk them through it.

When a player finishes, the app sends **you** a little notice that they want to
join. You can wait for all of them, or approve them one at a time as they trickle
in — either is fine.

---

## Step 5 — Approve each player

When a player has asked to join, you'll see their request on your **dashboard**
or **My Teams** (there's a little notification when one is waiting for you).

Each request leads with the player's **name**, then asks you to place them:

- **If you pre-made placeholder names** for your team, you'll see them listed —
  *"Is this one of your players?"* Tap the matching name to **connect** them
  (that keeps their stats and history). If they're new, tap **"Not one of these
  — just add them to the team."**
- **If you didn't pre-make names**, it's a single **"Add to the team"** button.
- **Decline** — only if it's someone who should *not* be on your team.

That's it — once you add or connect them, they're on the team and get a "You're
on the team!" message on their phone. Do this for each player as requests come in.

When everyone's been added, you're done. Your roster is full.

---

## About placeholder names (if you pre-made them)

Some captains type in their roster names ahead of time (these are called
**placeholders** — a name with no account yet). Players **don't** pick their own
placeholder when they join — the share link is the same for everyone and doesn't
know who's who. They just **ask to join**, and **you** connect them to the right
name at approval. That keeps it in your hands and means two people can't
accidentally grab the same name.

**Rule of thumb:** if you made a placeholder for someone, tap their name to
**connect** them (rather than "just add"), so their stats carry over and you
don't end up with two of the same person on your roster.

---

## Something went wrong?

- **A player says the link doesn't work** → it may have been regenerated. In the
  "Invite my team" box there's a *"Link shared with the wrong people? Generate a
  new one"* option — if you used that, the old link stops working. Send the new
  one.
- **You don't see a request** → make sure the player finished all the way to
  tapping **"Add me"** — signing in alone doesn't send the request.
- **Wrong person asked to join** → tap **Decline**.

---

*That's the whole flow: share the message → approve each player. Everything else
the app handles.*
