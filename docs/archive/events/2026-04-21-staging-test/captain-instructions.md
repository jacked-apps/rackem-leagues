## Getting your players into the app

Ed has already added you as captain. You'll add each of your players as a **placeholder** — that's just a player record with no email attached yet.

### Step 1 — Open your team roster

1. Log in → **My Teams**
2. Expand your team card
3. Tap **Edit Team** (pencil icon)
4. You'll see empty roster slots

### Step 2 — Create the player

1. Click an empty roster slot
2. Click **Add Placeholder Player**
3. Fill in: first name, last name, city, state
4. Nickname auto-fills if you leave it blank (or type your own)
5. Leave starting handicaps alone unless you know better — defaults are fine
6. **Email:** skip it tonight (we'll cover this below)
7. Save — player is now on your roster

### Step 3 — Invite them to claim their account

Once the placeholder exists, tap the player's name on the roster. You'll see a popup menu. Tap **Register Player**. That opens the invite modal with four options — **tonight use one of these two:**

#### Option A — Device Handoff (easiest in person)
1. In the invite modal, pick **Device Handoff**
2. Hand your phone to the player
3. They set a password on the spot — done

#### Option B — Share Link / QR code (faster for a line of people)
1. In the invite modal, pick **Share Link**
2. Either copy the link OR show them the QR code on your screen
3. They scan with their phone, register on their own device — done

Either way, the player ends up registered and linked to the placeholder you created.

## GOTCHAS — read these

1. **You can't remove a placeholder once you add them.** If you typo a name, text Ed. Double-check before saving.
2. **No email = one team only.** If a player might end up on a second team this season, add their email when creating the placeholder. Skip this tonight — we're only testing one team per player.
3. **Invite links expire** in about a week. Not a problem tonight, but FYI.
4. **No duplicate warnings.** If another captain already added "John Smith," creating another John Smith makes two separate records. Search first by typing the name in the roster slot before clicking "Add Placeholder."
5. **Starting handicaps default to 0 (3v3) and 40 (5v5).** Don't sweat these tonight.
