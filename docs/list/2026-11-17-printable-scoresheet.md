Ed, 2026-11-17: scoring is paperless and should stay that way, so this is
very low priority — "almost not worth doing". The one case it covers is a
night the internet is down: an LO (or a player) could print a blank sheet
for the night's matchup ahead of time and enter the results later.

Today there is no way to print a scoresheet at all, and no paper sheets
exist for past seasons.

## The idea

A "Print scoresheet" option for a scheduled match: team names, lineup
slots, the game-by-game grid and the handicap targets, laid out for paper.
Results would still be entered in the app afterwards (LO manual scoring
already exists for that).
